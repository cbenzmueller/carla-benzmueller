Introduction

About me

My name is Carla and I live in Berlin. I love sports, particularly field hockey and running. I started playing hockey around 2018/19 when I joined the Zehlendorfer Wespen, and I have been a member of the Berlin state selection squad (Landeskader) in my age group in 2022, 2023 and again since January 2026.

From 2023 to 2025 I lived in Dubai. During that time I focused primarily on running, especially distances from 800 m to 5 km, competing for Ultimate Athletics Dubai and Abu Dhabi Athletics Club while being affiliated with Silvesterlauf Trier in Germany. I quickly reached the top of the national rankings in both countries: I hold three youth records of Rhineland-Palatinate and five of the Rhineland, have been listed among the top 50 of the German athletics rankings many times since 2023, and was selected for the athletics squad (Landeskader) of Rhineland-Palatinate. A newspaper report on my time in Dubai is here (in German).

After returning to Germany in autumn 2025 I put hockey first again. In October 2026 we won the German championship with the Wespen's girls' U16 — after the Berlin title, an intermediate round and a penalty-shootout semi-final at the Youth Finals in Mönchengladbach. Also take a look at the sites of my twin sister Elena and my brother Max, who are both into sports.

Zehlendorfer Wespen wU16 — German champions 2026

Berlin · field season 2026

The road to the title ran through three rounds. We finished the Berlin main round third and won the Berlin final round (6:0 in the final against Berliner HC); as the East's No. 1 seed we won intermediate round C against Rot-Weiss Köln and MTV Braunschweig; and at the Youth Finals in Mönchengladbach we beat HTC Uhlenhorst Mülheim in a penalty shootout and Münchner SC in the final. It was the Wespen's first German title in this age group and the first girls' U16 field final for both clubs in this millennium.
